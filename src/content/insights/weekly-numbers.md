Most owners check their bank balance and have a feeling about how the week went. That's a start, but these six numbers tell you much more.

**Sales this week compared with last week.** The weekly trend shows problems early, while there's still time to act.

**Cash in the bank and cash coming in.** Money owed to you isn't money you have. Look at both, and at what's due out in the next few weeks.

**New enquiries and where they came from.** Website, WhatsApp, referrals, walk-ins. When you know which source brings the most customers, you know where to spend your time.

**How many enquiries became customers.** If enquiries are steady but sales are falling, look at how you follow up before you spend more on marketing.

**Your best-selling and slowest products or services.** This tells you what to promote, what to stock and what to drop.

**Customers who came back.** Repeat customers usually cost less to win than new ones. If this number is falling, it's worth asking why.

You don't need special software to start. A simple sheet updated every Friday works. Once that becomes a habit, a dashboard that pulls these numbers from your sales and payment systems saves the time and removes the mistakes.
