You don't need an agency to tell you whether your website is working. Open it on your phone and check these five things.

**It's slow on mobile data.** Load your homepage on your phone using mobile data, not Wi-Fi. If it takes more than a few seconds, many visitors will leave before it finishes. Large photos are the usual cause, and they're easy to fix.

**People can't tell what you do.** Show your homepage to someone who doesn't know your business for five seconds, then ask what you sell and who it's for. If they can't say, the top of the page needs rewriting.

**There's no obvious next step.** Every page should make it easy to call, message, book or buy. If the contact details are only on a contact page, you're making people search.

**It doesn't work well on a phone.** Buttons too small to tap, text you have to zoom to read, forms that are painful to fill in. Most of your visitors are on phones, so this is where the site is judged.

**The information is out of date.** Old prices, last year's events, a phone number that no longer works. Visitors notice, and it makes them wonder what else is out of date. If updating the site means calling a developer, it's worth changing how the site is built so your team can do it.

If you found two or more of these, your website is probably losing you enquiries. Most of them can be fixed without starting again.
